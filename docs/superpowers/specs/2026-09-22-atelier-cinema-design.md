# Atelier Cinema — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Brand:** Sapataria Paulista  
**Approved direction:** Atelier Cinema (bold cinematic upgrade, keep dark / gold / serif DNA)

## Intent

Elevate the existing premium site into a cinematic atelier experience. Keep structure and visual language; replace stock feel with real photography, make Thiago Landes unmistakably the face of the brand, and modernize typography, motion, and copy across the full site.

## Decisions Locked

- Hero: atelier atmosphere first (not Thiago as background)
- Immediately after hero: large Thiago Landes block with photo **147**
- Story tone: origin (Paulista / engraxate) + mastery (atelier / grifes) in balance
- Scope: full site (home, sobre, serviços, processo, galeria, contato)
- Intensity: bold cinematic rewrite within current DNA
- Wife photo: **out of scope** until provided

## Visual System

- Keep: dark mode default, gold accent (`--accent`), Playfair Display + Inter, ultra-wide labels
- Refine: larger serif headlines, tighter supporting copy, more full-bleed imagery, fewer bordered “cards”
- Motion: slower reveals, image scale-on-hover, subtle ken-burns on hero, stagger cascades
- Avoid: purple themes, cream/terracotta AI defaults, pill clusters, glow, floating badges on hero
